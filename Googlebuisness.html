A few answers

Good to know before you begin.

Have another question? Send us a message and we'll help you understand the process.

Is ₹599 a monthly fee?

No. The TapCraves package is a one-time ₹599 payment. The package includes the listed setup and QR board service.

Will you guarantee Google ranking or verification?

No. Google controls profile approval, verification and search rankings. We help prepare the profile and guide you through the available process, but cannot guarantee Google's decisions or a particular ranking.

Can the QR board open my Google review page?

Yes. We prepare the QR destination using your actual Google review link. You can also provide social media links to include in the QR experience. A working customer-specific QR requires your real links.

What information should I share?

Typically your business name, category, address or service area, phone number, opening hours, and relevant social media or review links. We'll tell you what is needed for your case.

Is TapCraves affiliated with Google?

No. TapCraves is an independent service provider and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Google.
